Research & Projects

Autonomous Robot Obstacle Avoidance using Depth Sensing

Existential Robotics Laboratory (ERL), Dept. of ECE, PI: Prof. Nikolay Atanasov. UC San Diego
  • Simulated in PyBullet and Gazebo to verify whether obstacle avoidance autonomous vehicle can be achieved by combining the conversion of 3D images from a depth camera into a 2D costmap with the AStar and Time Elastic Band (TEB) algorithm.
  • Applied simulation results to a real race car and used the Adaptive Monte Carlo Localization (AMCL) method to ensure both systems operated correctly.
Apr 2022 - Apr 2023

Rescue Robot with ASLAM, Object Detection and Distributed Computing

Designed and built an autonomous rescue robot for disaster zones capable of exploration, mapping, victim detection, and supply delivery.

  • Integrated Gmapping, Karto, and ASLAM-based autonomous exploration.
  • Used ROS navigation with Dijkstra global planning and TEB local planning.
  • Deployed MobileNet SSD with a depth camera for lightweight casualty detection.
