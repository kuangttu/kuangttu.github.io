Research & Projects

June 2023 - Aug 2023

Autonomous Robot Obstacle Avoidance using Depth Sensing

Existential Robotics Laboratory (ERL), Dept. of ECE, PI: Prof. Nikolay Atanasov. UC San Diego
  • Simulated in PyBullet and Gazebo to verify whether obstacle avoidance autonomous vehicle can be achieved by combining the conversion of 3D images from a depth camera into a 2D costmap with the AStar and Time Elastic Band (TEB) algorithm.
  • Applied simulation results to a real race car and used the Adaptive Monte Carlo Localization (AMCL) method to ensure both systems operated correctly.
Paper
Sep 2022 - Apr 2023

Rescue Robot with Autonomous Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (ASLAM), Object Detection and Distributed Computing

Laboratory for Reliable Computing, Dept. of ECE, PI: Prof. Hsi-Pin Ma NTHU
  • Built an autonomous vehicle to assist in disaster relief using LiDAR and a depth camera to construct a 3D map of the disaster area.
  • Reduced the vehicle's GPU load through distributed computing, which allowed minimizing the size of the autonomous vehicle by eliminating the need for high-performance hardware.
  • Trained an object detection model to identify human body parts, aiding in the detection of partially buried victims in disaster areas.
Paper
Oct 2023 - Dec 2023

Analysis of Basketball Free Throw Techniques Using IMU and Image Recognition

PI: Prof. Hsi-Pin Ma Dept. of ECE, Prof. Wen-Hsin Chiu Dept. of Kinesiology NTHU
  • Constructed a low-cost device for analyzing free throw posture using an inertial measurement unit (IMU), Arduino Nano, SD card module, object recognition model, and 3D-printed components.
  • Captured hand trajectory with IMU and recorded the physical parameters of ball, release speed, and angle using ball detection model.
Paper

June 2022

Asia-Pacific Mechanics Contest for College Students (Race car's hub design)

STAM (Society of Theoretical and Applied Mechanics) Taiwan
  • Designed a racing wheel hub set capable of withstanding maximum pressure while minimizing weight, with the goal of maximizing the down-force/weight ratio.
  • Simulated the downward pressure applied to the 3D model on the computer and used the Topology function in Altair Inspire to remove excess unstressed parts to yield a design with optimal down-force/weight ratio.
